Our approach

Integrated by design, small by choice

Large agents split a building between a property manager, a facilities team and a client-finance desk in three cities. We put the three roles in one team and one phone call.

We take on a small number of buildings we can visit, in Surrey and the South East. That keeps operating costs down for owners, keeps relationships with occupiers personal, and keeps the building a good place to run a business.

The service charge year

Three moments an occupier should never be surprised by

The year runs from 1 April to 31 March. Here is what lands on your desk, and when.

Before 1 April

The budget pack

Issued ahead of the year, not after it. Every cost code has a plain-English note: what the money buys, how often, and why it changed.

  • Total for the building and cost per sq ft
  • Your share, by floor area, with any lease cap applied
  • Any line moving more than 5% explained
Quarterly

Demands and updates

Quarterly demands that match the budget. Empty units and capped leases are the landlord's cost, never spread across the other occupiers.

  • VAT shown separately where the building is elected
  • Helpdesk log of what was reported and fixed
  • Notice of any planned works before they start
After 31 March

Reconciliation

Actual spend against budget, reviewed by an independent accountant, with a balancing credit or charge and the working behind it.

  • Line-by-line actuals against budget
  • Procurement record for every contract
  • Questions answered before the next budget is set
